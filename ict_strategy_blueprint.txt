V6 MASTER PRO — ICT METHOD 2
============================

English description
-------------------
Session-based liquidity framework. The setup starts with consolidation and
the dealing range, then studies the final minutes of the active session for
displacement, a liquidity sweep, and a return to fair value.

Rules
-----
1. Mark the consolidation range and dealing range before expansion.
2. Define the active session high, low, open, and equilibrium.
3. Analyze the final minutes of the active session, not random candles.
4. Require a clear displacement candle after a liquidity sweep.
5. Confirm that the sweep takes external liquidity and rejects back inside.
6. Enter on the return to the fair value area / imbalance.
7. Invalidate beyond the sweep extreme.
8. Target opposing liquidity or the next dealing-range extreme.

اردو وضاحت
-----------
پہلے consolidation اور dealing range mark کریں۔ active session کے آخری
minutes میں liquidity sweep اور displacement candle تلاش کریں۔ sweep کے بعد
price fair value area میں واپس آئے تو entry لیں۔ sweep extreme کے باہر close
ہو جائے تو setup ختم سمجھیں۔
