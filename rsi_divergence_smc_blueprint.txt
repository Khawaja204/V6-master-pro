V6 MASTER PRO — RSI DIVERGENCE + SMC SCALP 4
=============================================

English description
-------------------
Short-timeframe reversal model combining hidden RSI divergence with a market
structure shift. The scalp remains tight and is invalidated by a fresh swing
break.

Rules and levels
----------------
Hidden bullish divergence: price makes a higher low while RSI makes a lower
low, preferably with RSI in the 30–40 zone.
Hidden bearish divergence: price makes a lower high while RSI makes a higher
high, preferably with RSI above 50.
Structure shift: a confirmed break in the expected reversal direction.
Entry: divergence + structure shift + liquidity/SMC confirmation.
Invalidation: fresh swing break against the position.
Target: nearest internal liquidity, then external liquidity if momentum holds.

اردو وضاحت
-----------
Hidden bullish divergence میں price higher low بنائے مگر RSI lower low بنائے،
خاص طور پر RSI 30 سے 40 کے درمیان۔ Hidden bearish divergence میں price lower
high بنائے مگر RSI 50 سے اوپر ہو۔ صرف divergence پر trade نہ لیں؛ market
structure shift لازمی ہے۔ fresh swing break ہو تو scalp invalidate ہے۔
