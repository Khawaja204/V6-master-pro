V6 MASTER PRO — PRICE ACTION / SCALP-INTRADAY
=============================================

English description
-------------------
Scalp and intraday framework built from the 4H open-close direction, 1H
support and resistance, order blocks, fair value gaps, RSI context, and
Coinglass liquidation/liquidity heatmaps.

Rules and formulas
------------------
4H direction = bullish when 4H Close > 4H Open; bearish when 4H Close < 4H Open.
Support      = confirmed 1H swing-low reaction zone.
Resistance   = confirmed 1H swing-high reaction zone.
Risk/Reward  = (Target - Entry) / (Entry - Invalidation).
Target band  = 1% to 3% from entry, selected by the nearest valid liquidity.
Heatmap     = inspect both 12h and 24h liquidity/liquidation concentrations.

Entry rules
-----------
1. Use the 4H open-close direction as the session anchor.
2. Map valid 1H support and resistance before taking a trade.
3. If 1H support/resistance is not valid, do not call the level an Order Block
   or Fair Value Gap; skip the setup until structure is confirmed.
4. Validate the direction against the 12h and 24h Coinglass heatmaps.
5. Skip an entry when a liquidation cluster sits directly against the trade.
6. RSI uptrend Buy signal: look for RSI holding/reclaiming around 30 with
   price structure confirmation.
7. RSI downtrend Buy signal: only consider an exhaustion reversal when RSI is
   between 15 and 25 and a bullish structure shift confirms it.
8. Sell setups require a resistance rejection, bearish structure, and no
   opposing liquidity sweep.

اردو وضاحت
-----------

درست طور پر mark کریں۔ اگر 1H level valid نہیں تو اسے OB یا FVG نہ سمجھیں۔
12h اور 24h Coinglass heatmap میں مخالف liquidity ہو تو trade چھوڑ دیں۔
Uptrend میں RSI تقریباً 30 کے قریب Buy signal دے سکتا ہے، جبکہ downtrend
میں صرف 15 سے 25 RSI exhaustion کے بعد structure shift پر reversal دیکھیں۔
