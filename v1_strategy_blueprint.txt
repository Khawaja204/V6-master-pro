V6 MASTER PRO — V1 SMOOTHED HEIKIN ASHI + RSI 55
==================================================

English description
-------------------
Trend-following setup using Smoothed Heikin Ashi candles, a 30-minute entry
chart, and a 1-day structure filter. The trade is valid only when the lower
timeframe signal agrees with the higher timeframe direction.

Core formulas
-------------
Heikin Ashi Close  = (Open + High + Low + Close) / 4
Heikin Ashi Open   = (Previous HA Open + Previous HA Close) / 2
Heikin Ashi High   = max(High, HA Open, HA Close)
Heikin Ashi Low    = min(Low, HA Open, HA Close)
Smoothed HA        = EMA(HA value, smoothing length)
RSI                = 100 - (100 / (1 + Average Gain / Average Loss))

Buy rules
---------
1. The 1D structure is bullish or has reclaimed a major support level.
2. The 30m Smoothed Heikin Ashi candle closes bullish in the trend direction.
3. RSI is above 55 and rising; RSI 55 is the momentum gate.
4. Volume and price direction pass the VSA check.
5. Mark entry only after candle close confirmation.

Sell / invalidation rules
-------------------------
1. Reject a long when the 1D structure is bearish.
2. Invalidate when the entry candle wick breaks the protected swing level.
3. Do not enter during a contradictory VSA manipulation signal.
4. A failed reclaim or close back below the mapped level cancels the setup.

Risk and target
---------------
Entry: confirmed 30m close after all filters agree.
Invalidation: protected wick / swing break.
Take profit: 1% from entry for the standard V1 setup.
Risk rule: never move invalidation farther away after entry.
