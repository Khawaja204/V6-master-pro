V6 MASTER PRO — VOLUME SPREAD ANALYSIS
======================================

English description
-------------------
VSA compares price direction with relative traded volume and spread behavior.
The desk separates healthy confirmation from absorption, distribution, and
possible manipulation.

Checks
------
1. Volume increase + price increase: healthy demand / accumulation candidate.
2. Volume decrease + price decrease: weak participation; wait for clarity.
3. Volume increase + price decrease: possible manipulation Buy / absorption;
   require a demand-zone reclaim before entry.
4. Volume decrease + price increase: possible distribution / weak rally;
   check for a Sell setup and liquidity sweep.
5. Confirm spread, close location, and next-candle follow-through.
6. A single abnormal candle is not enough; require structure confirmation.
7. Never trade directly into a large opposing liquidation cluster.

Relative-volume formula
-----------------------
Relative Volume % = ((Current Volume / Reference Volume) - 1) * 100
Reference Volume should be the same timeframe's rolling median or mean.
Manipulation flag = opposing price/volume direction plus abnormal relative
volume and a failed follow-through candle.

اردو وضاحت
-----------
Volume اور price دونوں بڑھیں تو healthy demand ہو سکتی ہے۔ Volume بڑھے مگر
price گرے تو absorption یا manipulation Buy zone ہو سکتا ہے، لیکن demand
zone reclaim کے بغیر entry نہ لیں۔ Price بڑھے مگر volume کم ہو تو distribution
کا خطرہ ہے۔ ہر signal کو structure اور اگلی candle سے confirm کریں۔
